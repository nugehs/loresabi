# LoreSabi by Bashbop - Product Brief

## One-Line Pitch

Understand any country through simple visual explainers covering history, culture, current affairs, pop culture, and what people are searching.

## Product Idea

LoreSabi by Bashbop is a mobile-first web product that helps people understand countries without needing to read long articles, academic history, or confusing news coverage.

Users can search questions like:

- Why is Nigeria called Nigeria?
- Did Nigeria have another flag before?
- What does Ghana's Black Star mean?
- Why are people protesting in France?
- What does japa mean?
- What are people searching in Japan today?

The product returns structured explainers with short answers, images, timelines, context, related questions, and sources.

## Target Users

- Diaspora communities reconnecting with heritage
- Travellers preparing for a country
- Students learning global context
- Curious people who want simple explanations
- People following current affairs but missing background context
- People dating, working, studying, or moving across cultures

## Core Value

Most news apps explain what happened. Most country apps show static facts. LoreSabi explains what shaped a country and what people are curious about now.

## Product Principles

- Start with the simple answer.
- Show sources and last updated dates.
- Use visuals, timelines, and cards instead of walls of text.
- Separate fact, opinion, uncertainty, and speculation.
- Keep AI behind the scenes, not as the main user interface.
- Make the product feel useful, trustworthy, and culturally aware.

## MVP Countries

- Nigeria
- Ghana
- United Kingdom
- United States
- South Africa

Nigeria should be the first launch country.

## MVP Categories

- Name origin
- Flags and national symbols
- History timeline
- Culture and languages
- Current affairs
- Pop culture
- Search trends

## Monetization Direction

Keep basic search and explainers free. Add paid depth later:

- Saved country packs
- Offline reading
- Audio briefings
- Diaspora guides
- School/student packs
- Advanced trend alerts
- API/licensing for publishers or educators
