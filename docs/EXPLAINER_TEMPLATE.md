## User Question

Write the question in the language a normal person would search.

Example: `How did Nigeria get its name?`

## Short Answer

One paragraph. Answer first, avoid throat-clearing, and keep it easy to quote.

## Why It Matters

Explain why the question is bigger than trivia. Connect it to identity, history, culture, politics, language, migration, or public memory.

## Context

Write the deeper explanation in plain English. Use short paragraphs. Avoid turning the explainer into an academic essay.
