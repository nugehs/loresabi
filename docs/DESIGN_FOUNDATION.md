# LoreSabi by Bashbop - Design Foundation

## Direction

LoreSabi should use the UK Government Digital Service approach as a foundation for clarity, accessibility, and service design.

This does not mean copying the GOV.UK visual brand. It means adopting the underlying product discipline:

- Start with user needs.
- Do the hard work to make complex information simple.
- Use plain language.
- Make every page easy to scan.
- Make interactions predictable.
- Design for everyone, including people with lower confidence, lower literacy, accessibility needs, or limited context.
- Be consistent, not uniform.
- Build a useful service, not just a website.

## What This Means For LoreSabi

LoreSabi should feel:

- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Useful
- Accessible
- Easy to understand
- Culturally aware without becoming noisy

It should not feel:

- Like a generic AI chatbot
- Like a cluttered news site
- Like an academic database
- Like a government service visually copied without soul
- Like a social feed full of distraction

## Product Rules

- Put the short answer first.
- Explain why the topic matters.
- Show sources clearly.
- Use timelines when dates matter.
- Avoid vague labels like "learn more" when a clearer action exists.
- Keep search prominent.
- Make country pages scannable before they become deep.
- Use strong focus states and accessible contrast.
- Treat mobile users as first-class, even though the product is web-first.

## Visual Rules

- Use generous white space, but keep information dense enough for repeat use.
- Use cards for individual content items, not whole page sections.
- Use tabs, filters, and lists where they reduce confusion.
- Use country imagery, maps, flags, and sourced visuals where they add context.
- Avoid decorative visuals that do not teach anything.
- Keep typography direct and readable.
